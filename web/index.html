Measured, not claimed

Method

A calorie count from a photograph is a guess wearing a lab coat. Here is exactly what this does, and what it refuses to pretend.

01

It sees, then hedges

A pretrained Food-101 classifier reads the photo and returns a ranked list with real confidence scores. If the top guess is below 40%, the interface asks instead of asserting.

02

Ranges, never a single figure

A photograph cannot weigh food. Every macro is shown as a band (±20%) and the portion is yours to set. A number without an error bar is a false precision.

03

Corrections are counted

When you overrule the model, it is logged. The Patterns view reports how often that happened — the app measures its own trustworthiness.
